## What Are Electrical Control Cabinet Components?

Electrical control cabinet components are the mechanical and electrical parts mounted inside an enclosure to support, connect and protect control equipment. A typical panel combines structural parts (rails, brackets, enclosures), connection parts (terminal blocks, jumpers, ferrules) and routing parts (cable ducts, conduit, ties, strain reliefs).

Choosing the right mix matters because the cabinet is the nervous system of any automated machine. Poorly specified parts lead to wiring errors, heat buildup, maintenance downtime and non-compliance with electrical standards. Our job is to make that specification simple and repeatable.
