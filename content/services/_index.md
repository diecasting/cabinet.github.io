## How We Support Your Projects

We provide more than parts. Our services are designed to shorten your supply chain and de-risk your panel production.

### Kit Configuration
Send us your panel drawing or BOM and we return a validated component kit — rails cut to length, terminals sorted, hardware bagged by zone.

### Private-Label & OEM / ODM
We develop branded component kits and custom parts under confidentiality agreements, including tooling, prototyping and volume production.

### Global Logistics
Consolidated shipments, neutral packaging and documentation (CoC, RoHS/REACH declarations) simplify import and assembly worldwide.

### Engineering Support
Our application engineers help you select the right DIN rail components, terminal blocks and cable management parts for your enclosure and standards.
