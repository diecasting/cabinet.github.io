## Industrial Control Cabinet Parts

When a machine goes down, the control cabinet is often where the fault is found. That is why specifiers choose **industrial control cabinet parts** built for repeatability and serviceability rather than the cheapest commodity option. We manufacture the structural, connection and routing parts that make a panel safe and maintainable.

From a single replacement terminal to a full production run of enclosure hardware, our catalog covers the parts industrial panels depend on every day.

## The Three Layers of a Control Cabinet

A reliable cabinet is built from three part layers:

1. **Structural** — DIN rails, mounting brackets, end stops and plates that hold everything in place.
2. **Connection** — terminal blocks, jumpers, ferrules and markers that carry and identify signals and power.
3. **Routing** — cable ducts, conduit, clamps, ties and strain reliefs that keep wiring organized.

Getting all three right is what separates a panel that is easy to service from one that becomes a tangle. Our [Products](/products/) page groups parts along these lines.

## Structural Parts

The backbone of any panel is the mounting system. We produce:

- **DIN rails** — TH35 top-hat and G32 G-type in steel, aluminum and stainless.
- **Brackets and end stops** — to secure rails and prevent creep.
- **Mounting plates** — for heavy devices and transformers.
- **Grounding parts** — to maintain enclosure equipotential bonding.

For the full mounting range, see [DIN Rail Components Manufacturer](/din-rail-components-manufacturer/).

## Connection Parts

Terminals are where most panel faults originate, so we focus on consistent quality:

- **Screw terminal blocks** — robust for high-vibration panels.
- **Spring-cage terminals** — maintenance-free, vibration resistant.
- **Push-in terminals** — fastest wiring for series production.
- **Jumpers, markers and partition plates** — for clear, configurable layouts.

Each type is available with matching accessories so the panel can be reconfigured without special tools.

## Routing Parts

Neat wiring is safe wiring. Our cable management range includes:

- **Cable ducts** — slotted, with snap-on covers.
- **Flexible conduit** — for moving axes and field devices.
- **Cable clamps and ties** — for fixed routing.
- **Strain reliefs** — to protect cables at entry points.

## Enclosure Hardware

Small parts cause big delays. We supply screws, standoffs, washers, locking nuts and grounding bars in corrosion-resistant finishes, bagged and labeled so they are easy to find on the bench.

## Built for Harsh Environments

Not every cabinet lives in a clean room. For food, chemical, marine or outdoor installs we offer stainless and aluminum parts with appropriate surface treatment, and we can advise on material selection for your environment.

## Quality and Traceability

Industrial parts must be trustworthy. Our ISO 9001 managed process includes incoming inspection, dimensional and torque checks, and electrical continuity testing where relevant. Certificates of conformity and RoHS/REACH declarations are available, and lots are traceable end to end.
