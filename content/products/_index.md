## Core Product Categories

We manufacture and supply a complete portfolio of electrical control cabinet components engineered for panel builders, OEMs and automation integrators. Every part is selected to work together as a system, reducing design time and assembly errors.

### 1. Control Cabinet Component Kits
Pre-configured kits bundle DIN rails, terminal blocks, circuit breakers, fuses and wiring accessories into a single, ready-to-assemble package. Kits are available for standard panel sizes and can be customized to your bill of materials.

### 2. Electrical Installation Accessories
Cable glands, ferrules, wire markers, end sleeves and termination hardware that support clean, code-compliant installations in any industrial environment.

### 3. DIN Rail Components
Top-hat (TH35) and G-type (G32) rails in steel, aluminum and stainless steel, plus end stops, brackets, markers and mounting hardware.

### 4. Terminal Block Accessories
Screw, spring-cage and push-in terminal blocks with jumpers, partition plates, marking strips and test accessories.

### 5. Cable Management Parts
Cable ducts, flexible conduit, clamps, cable ties, strain reliefs and routing hardware that keep wiring organized and protected.
