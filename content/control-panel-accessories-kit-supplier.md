## Control Panel Accessories Kit Supplier

As an experienced **control panel accessories kit supplier**, we turn the scattered list of small parts behind every electrical enclosure into one tidy, repeatable package. Panel builders waste significant time sourcing rails, terminals, ducts, glands and markers from multiple vendors. We consolidate that into a single kit — pre-configured for standard panels or custom-built around your drawing.

Our kits reduce picking errors, shorten assembly time and give purchasing a single line item instead of dozens. That is why OEMs, panel shops and automation integrators across industrial automation and machine building choose us as their kit partner.

## Why a Kit Instead of Loose Parts?

A control panel is assembled from hundreds of small components. Buying them loose creates hidden costs:

- Multiple purchase orders and suppliers to manage.
- Inconsistent stock and long lead times.
- Picking mistakes on the bench that cause rework.
- No single point of accountability for quality.

A kit solves all four. Rails arrive cut, terminals arrive sorted, and hardware is bagged by zone with a clear build sheet. The assembler opens one box and builds.

## What Our Kits Contain

Depending on your panel, a [Control Panel Accessories Kit](/products/) may include:

- **DIN rails** — TH35 top-hat and G32 rails in steel, aluminum or stainless.
- **Terminal blocks** — screw, spring-cage and push-in types with jumpers and markers.
- **Cable management** — ducts, flexible conduit, clamps, ties and strain reliefs.
- **Wiring accessories** — ferrules, end sleeves, wire markers and test accessories.
- **Enclosure hardware** — screws, standoffs, mounting plates and grounding parts.

For the mounting layer specifically, see our [DIN Rail Components Manufacturer](/din-rail-components-manufacturer/) page.

## Pre-Configured vs Custom Kits

We offer two routes:

1. **Pre-configured kits** for common panel sizes — fast to order, ready to ship.
2. **Custom kits** built from your drawing or BOM — rails cut to length, terminals counted, hardware bagged by zone.

Both ship with a build sheet so assembly is consistent across batches and sites. Custom kits are ideal for OEMs who want every machine panel identical.

## Quality You Can Trace

Every kit is assembled under an ISO 9001 managed process. Incoming material is inspected, in-process gauging checks dimensions and torque, and final functional checks confirm continuity where relevant. On request we provide:

- Certificates of conformity (CoC)
- RoHS and REACH declarations
- Full lot traceability

This documentation supports your own quality and compliance records, especially for export markets.
