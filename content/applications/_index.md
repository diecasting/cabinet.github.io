## Application Examples

Our components appear in control panels and enclosures across industrial and commercial installations.

### Automation Panels
DIN rail mounting components, terminal blocks and cable ducts form the backbone of PLC and drive cabinets.

### Machine Control
Push-in terminals and compact accessories keep OEM machine panels serviceable and space-efficient.

### Power Distribution
Enclosure accessories and wiring hardware support safe, organized low-voltage distribution.

### Building Management
Control systems for HVAC, lighting and access use our reliable terminal and cable management parts.

### Renewable Energy
Inverters and combiner boxes use our stainless and aluminum DIN rail components for corrosion resistance.
