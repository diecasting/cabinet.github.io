## Electrical Enclosure Accessories

An enclosure is more than a box. The **electrical enclosure accessories** inside and around it determine whether a control cabinet is safe to open, easy to mount and protected from dust, moisture and interference. We supply the glands, plates, grounding parts and hardware that complete a professional panel.

Panel builders and OEMs specify our accessories because they are consistent, documented and available in the same kit as the rails and terminals.

## Cable Entry and Strain Relief

Most enclosure failures start at the cable entry. Our range covers:

- **Cable glands** — for round, shielded and armored cables, with sealing and strain relief.
- **Multi-hole plates** — for neat, sealed entries.
- **Strain reliefs** — to protect cables at the wall.
- **Conduit fittings** — for flexible routing to field devices.

Materials include plastic, nickel-plated brass and stainless for corrosive or outdoor installs.

## Mounting and Internal Hardware

Inside the cabinet, devices need a secure home:

- **Mounting plates** — for heavy equipment and transformers.
- **DIN rails and brackets** — see our [DIN Rail Components Manufacturer](/din-rail-components-manufacturer/).
- **Standoffs and screws** — corrosion-resistant finishes.
- **Grounding bars and clips** — for equipotential bonding.

## Doors, Hinges and Locks

For accessible, secure enclosures we supply:

- **Hinges** — concealed and surface types.
- **Locks and latches** — with keys or tools.
- **Seals and gaskets** — to maintain ingress protection.

## Why Accessories Matter for Compliance

A panel that looks finished but lacks proper grounding, sealing or strain relief creates real risk. Our accessories are selected to support:

- Ingress protection at cable entries.
- Mechanical strain relief on conductors.
- Equipotential bonding through grounding parts.
- Traceable, documented materials.

This supports the broader [Industrial Control Cabinet Parts](/industrial-control-cabinet-parts/) quality story.
